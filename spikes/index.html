Dandelion Field — Phase 0 spikes

Throwaway prototypes to answer whether the inputs work. Nothing is sent anywhere.

  • Breath spike — mic → loudness, brightness, breathiness, breath detection, 20-byte fingerprint
  • Field spike — fake meadow of network neighbourhoods: visits grow fibers, breaths comb them and slowly relax, pinwheel strokes bend with the comb
  • Pinwheel spike — webcam → track a coloured mark → spin speed and direction
