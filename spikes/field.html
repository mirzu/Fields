Layout:

Visits grow fibers in your patch; they never go away. Colour = time of day of the visit (amber day, rose dawn/dusk, blue night); recent visits are brighter.
Breath combs fibers along its wind: strongest in its own patch, weaker in nearby patches of the clump. The gust plays out, then the lean slowly relaxes.
Pinwheel: streaming stroke along its wind, curled by spin, bent by local breath.
Zoomed out: fullness = visits, lean = recent breath, streaks = pinwheels.
Wind (dial, bottom right): midday and midnight blow opposite ways; dawn and dusk are calm.
